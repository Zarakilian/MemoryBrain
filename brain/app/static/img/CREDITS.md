# Image credits

Public-domain Leonardo da Vinci scans. Used as faint accents (per-lens empty
states, inspector watermark) and as ghosted plates on the ambient codex
layer — never at legibility-harming opacity.

- `vitruvian.jpg` — Vitruvian Man (Gallerie dell'Accademia, Venice).
- `flowers.jpg` — botanical study.
- `flying_machine.jpg` — design for a flying machine (Codex Atlanticus).
- `tuscan.jpg` — Tuscan landscape drawing.

The drifting line engravings (gears, moon phases, icosahedron, golden
spiral, bird-flight study, star chart, botanical sprig, water vortex, eye
study, wing ribs, mirrored script) are drawn procedurally by
`static/js/codex.js` in homage — they are not scans.
